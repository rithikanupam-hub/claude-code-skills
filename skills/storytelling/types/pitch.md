# Type: Investor / pitch film

**Job:** a prospective investor ends the film feeling "this grows, and I want in" without us saying so. Selling is the point, but it's **shown** through one person's story and real data, never claimed.
**Simulated viewer:** the actual investor profile. They watch once, sound on, and judge whether it's real.

## Spine (story.md)
- **One person** the investor can root for.
- **The problem** in one image (everyone watches the ball).
- **Who struggles today** (parent: "Com'è andata?" "Bene."; the mister with 22 kids; the player who doesn't know what to improve).
- **What we do + the product moment** with real data.
- **Why it grows:** the human loop (family shares it) → the market ("football grows").
- **Signature line** at the peak.

## Shape
Default frame: Story Circle compressed (you → need → go → find → change), or Strategy Story short form for a deck film. 45–60 s, 16:9. Music can be the timeline (beat grid, cuts on downbeats, the drop on the signature line). One visual rhyme and one transition language. One human peak (the kid's small smile). The hook frame already moves at frame 0; show 3 opening options before building the rest.

## Ending and selling
No spoken ask. The end card carries the brand mark and an "illustrative images" note if AI footage is used. Never claim someone shown is a customer.

## Story check: extra items
- Every number on screen is traceable to a file. Never generated data.
- Sound-off retell (3 sentences) says the three feelings.
- Brand rules from the project (what never to mention, how people may be shown, local vocabulary, screen text readable in 1 s).
- The investor-specific question is answered: why this, why now, why it grows.
