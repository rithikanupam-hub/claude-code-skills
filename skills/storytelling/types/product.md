# Type: Product / use-case explainer (incl. our own results and honest founder tests)

**Job:** the viewer sees the product work for someone like them, believes it, and knows the one next step. Selling is allowed. Lying, inflating and drawing fake UI are not.
**Examples:** `real-ai-receptionist`, `ai-concierge`, `seo-*` (Mission Control proof), customer-service-face, Footura explainer, badge feature.
**Simulated viewer:** the buyer role (a small-business owner, a marketer, a club president or parent for us), skimming on their phone.

## Spine
- **Who** and the outcome they want (named in line 1).
- **The moment** in their day where it goes wrong (pillar "AI use cases": one role, one task, one worked example; label hypothetical scenarios).
- **The demo = the proof:** real screen recording or our own measured result. The UI is never drawn; example app screens are labelled as examples.
- **Head fake still required:** name the viewer's assumption ("you'd think it's prejudice, but it's effort"; "doesn't Google punish AI?").
- **Next step:** one CTA that really exists (a repo, a keyword, a trial).

## Shape
Default frame: StoryBrand (viewer = hero, we = guide) or Strategy Story, full for MOFU/BOFU.
- The demo comes right after the hook (by 4–8 s), never after the explanation. Cut the explanation to one but/so turn.
- Any character is alive from frame 0 (idle loop), and the screen is never dark.
- Our own results: gather the full metric set first (all-time and monthly, impressions, clicks, queries, top-3/top-10) and build on the strongest true numbers. Honest means nothing false. It doesn't mean volunteering every weak number. Offer a long "detailed" cut too.
- Marketer audience: direct call-out voice. Who it's for → proof in numbers → a numbered "exact workflow" with practitioner terms. Smooth balanced lines ("Less typing. More deciding.") read as AI. Add a "you're falling behind" line. "You need to see this" without the subject in the same breath is a hard fail.
- **Honest founder test** variant (belief → test → observation → next step): record the input as one continuous take, check the system logs, and show what actually happened, including when it failed.

## Ending and selling
One CTA, placed before the brand end card (like "Comment MISSION"). Keep the product promise at the level the proof supports. Universal claims become a forecast or "the exact workflow". Date windows end where the data ends. Put a "free fix" line after a paid-plan line, never before it.

## Story check: extra items
- **User first:** user → content → fix → check of the final output against the user's need.
- Every product claim has proof we recorded. Secondary-source figures can't be claimed until verified.
- Fresh news that's only adjacent to the topic goes where it closes a loop, not in the hook (your-voice-every-language).
- Grounding is our setup, not the vendor's claim. Don't put words in a vendor's mouth.
- Demo questions are everyday ones (shared rule 11).
