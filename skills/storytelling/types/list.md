# Type: Tips / list / checklist reel

**Job:** the viewer gets a short list they can use today, saves it, and comments the keyword for the full version. It's useful before it's promotional.
**Examples:** `ai-meeting-translator-tips` (5 things to keep in mind), checklist reel "Before you trust a translator with a client call, try these tests".
**Simulated viewer:** an evaluator: someone who runs meetings with clients in another language.

## Spine
- Who it's for and the outcome they want (named in the first line).
- 3–7 items. Each one is a concrete action plus why it matters, in one or two lines.
- Proof per item from our own tests, shown as "what we saw", never as a product guarantee.

## Shape
Default frame: StoryBrand short form or Strategy Story (viewer = hero, we = guide). Item numbers sit on screen with readable holds. Loud / high-energy treatments are allowed (founder 2026-10-25, `ai-meeting-translator-tips/CONTEXT_CARD.md`: "we do loud"). Default to LinkedIn first; Instagram only if a story hook carries it.

## Ending and selling
One concrete task today + keyword ask ("Which one breaks your current setup?" / "Send us CHECKLIST"). The promised list must exist before posting. It's an evaluation framework, not a product performance claim.

## Story check: extra items
- **User first** (founder 2026-10-03): user → what goes wrong → fixes → a check against their need.
- Each item is testable by the viewer without our product.
- **Reach check:** the list works for anyone who joins a call in a second language, not only builders.
