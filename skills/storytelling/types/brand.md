# Type: Brand / origin story

**Job:** the viewer knows what we believe, where it came from, and why it's true for them. It's a story, never a feature list.
**Simulated viewer:** the brand's core audience.

## Spine
- **The founder's real moment** (taken from the interview, never invented) or the world's moment (machines talk in every language, but meetings still happen between people).
- **Why it matters to the viewer**, and **the signature line**.

## Shape
Default frame: hero's journey (founder origin only) or a three-act (setup → struggle → what changed). Use the "opening image ↔ final image" mirror. Brand film variants per channel (brand film, LinkedIn line, SEO title) are in hooks-and-stories.md.

## Ending and selling
End on the signature line and the brand mark. No CTA, no product claims beyond what's true today. the project: show value, not the problem stats (those go to campaigns/About).

## Story check: extra items
- Every biographical fact is confirmed by the founder (quote the interview line).
- The signature line is approved (check DECISIONS / the brand book).
- No AI-looking imagery. the project: always the complete V, never mention cameras.
