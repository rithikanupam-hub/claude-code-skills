# Type: Educational / history story

**Job:** the viewer finishes knowing something true and surprising about people, language or understanding, and repeats it to a friend. We teach, we never sell.
**Examples:** `FINALS/georgetown-ibm-1954`, `nuremberg-1945`, `ich-bin-ein-berliner`, `voder-1939`, `what-is-inclusion`, `tokyo-voice-ruling`, `carter-poland-1977`.
**Simulated viewer:** a 27-year-old scrolling Reels on the bus, who has never heard of the topic. On LinkedIn: a working professional.

## Spine (story.md)
- **Head fake:** what most people believe (A) vs the researched truth (B). No head fake means no video, so go back to research.
- **Character:** a real person, or a clearly labelled illustrative everyday person.
- **Stakes, big question, takeaway** (an insight, not our slogan).
- 5-line core (situation, desire, conflict, change, result); the head fake is the change line.

## Shape
Default frame: **dopamine loop** (`loop.md`) shaped as a Fichtean curve: stakes → big question → build-up → head fake → payoff → final question, with a smaller loop every 15–20 s. Length: 60–75 s (180–220 words) for YouTube/LinkedIn, 35–45 s for a reel. Sensitive topic: a quiet Freytag shape.
Bring the twist forward. Mechanism or background lines before the twist read as a lecture (voder: the twist moved from 32 s to ~20 s).

## Ending and selling
No product claims, no CTA except the end card. Say "we" only if the story is ours.
- **Every educational film except a documentary** (founder 2026-10-03, item 10): it ends with one concrete task the viewer can do today, then a useful keyword ask. The documentary exception came from the 2026-10-06 Tokyo lesson.
- **Documentary** (history, legal story): one narrator, clear chapters spoken in full ("Chapter one: the copy."), and it ends on a thought, never a task for the viewer.
- **How-to educational:** the same task + keyword ask ("Want the full checklist? Send us CHECKLIST"); the promised thing must exist before posting. It also follows the **real work order, user first** (who they are, what they type, what they need → the content → fix it to fit them → check the final output against their need; founder 2026-10-03, seo-international).

## Story check: extra items
- **Sources of any age** (founder, 2026-11-06): history, older data and documents are all fine here. The "last 2 months" rule is only for AI news films (`news.md`). If a recent event really fits, it can be the hook, but it isn't required.
- Myth films: the myth is stated before it's denied.
- Legal or long stories: spans, filing dates and caveats go to on-screen chips; the spoken line keeps to the story.

## Voice default
"Calm, curious documentary narrator. 175–185 wpm. Dry, warm, no hype. A pause before the head fake. The last question rises."
