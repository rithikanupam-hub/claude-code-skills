# Type: Office scene film (fictional situation)

**Job:** the viewer recognises their own team in a small everyday moment and asks the question themselves. Little or no narration lecture.
**Examples:** "The second job" (video-production-and-skills.md), "The missing thread" paper explainer.
**Simulated viewer:** someone who sits in multilingual meetings at work.

## Spine
Situation → interruption → consequence → question (hooks-and-stories.md). One room, 2–3 characters, one small cost (unfinished notes, a contribution that arrives too late).

## Shape
25–30 s. Beat table with visible action, camera and sound per 5–6 s. Observational, restrained camera, natural light. A metaphor is fine (paper thread), but say it's a metaphor, not measured behaviour.

## Ending and selling
End on an open question to the viewer ("Does your team rely on one colleague to translate?"). The product promise stays at exploration level ("We're exploring another way…") until proof exists.

## Story check: extra items
- Visible label "Fictional AI-generated scenario" in the film and the caption.
- No accents used as comedy, no nationality shorthand, no generated interface pretending to be the product, no fake triumphant before/after.
- Fictional until supported by a real account. Quotes from customers are kept apart from founder guesses.
