# Persona template (built around a decision)

## P{n}. {Role}: {primary audience | trust view | buyer | influencer | end user}
- **Question they bring:** the one thing they want to find out.
- **Positive outcome for them:** what counts as success for this persona. It is often not the site's main CTA (e.g. "wants to discuss it with a parent").
- **Authority:** what they can decide alone, and who else must agree.
- **Arrival:** source, device, moment, attention span.
- **Reading style and tech comfort.**
- **Scroll pace:** flick size in screens and pause in ms, for `scripts/walk.mjs --flick F --pause MS` (skimmer ~0.8 / 350, careful reader ~0.3 / 1500).
- **Worries:** what makes them stop or distrust.
- **What would convince them:** type of proof.
- **Colour (secondary):** name, age, town, family. Only enough to stay in character.

These are test hypotheses, not researched customer profiles. The primary-audience persona decides the home page verdict; the others reveal supporting or downstream issues.
